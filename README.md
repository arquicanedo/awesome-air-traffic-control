# Awesome Air Traffic Control

A list of air traffic control simulators, tools, and training software.

Most lists of this kind are about flying. This one is about the other side of the
radio: the software people use to work, practice, or study the controller's job.

## Scope

An entry belongs here if it is software a controller, a trainee, or someone curious
about the job would actually use or learn from. That covers simulators, radar
clients, data and navigation tooling, and structured training material.

Out of scope: flight simulators and pilot-side add-ons, real-time flight trackers,
and airline or airport management games where control is incidental.

Entries are alphabetical inside each section. That ordering is not a ranking, and
there are no scores, prices, or status grades here on purpose. Follow the link and
judge for yourself.

## Simulators

### Open source

- [Co-ATC](https://github.com/yegors/co-atc) - Air traffic control simulation project.
- [openScope](https://www.openscope.co/) - Browser-based radar simulator covering real airspace, with a community-maintained airport library.
- [Vice](https://pharr.org/vice/) - Radar client and simulator.

### Proprietary

- [Air Traffic Greenlight](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1861880/Air_Traffic_Greenlight/) - Spatial routing game built on real city geography and elevation data.
- [Airflow](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1693800/Airflow/) - Enroute and approach control across US ARTCCs.
- [Airwave](https://arwv.cc/) - Browser-based approach control.
- [Flight Level](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2105450/Flight_Level/) - Area and approach control.
- [Global ATC Simulator](https://store.steampowered.com/app/270830/Global_ATC_Simulator/) - Tower and approach control with worldwide airport coverage.
- [MultiScope](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4832540/MultiScope_Air_Traffic_Control/) - Approach control at Amsterdam Schiphol, with local voice recognition.
- [ProjectATC](https://www.projectatc.com/) - Air traffic control simulator.
- [Radar Identified](https://radar-identified.com/) - Enroute control in German airspace, browser-based.
- [SECTOR](https://sector.mx) - Approach and enroute control in Mexican and US airspace, with Spanish and English voice control.
- [vectorheavy](https://vectorheavy.com/play) - Browser-based vectoring practice.
- [VoiceATC Simulator](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3529560/VoiceATC_Simulator/) - Tower and approach control driven by local speech recognition.

## Tools and data

- [runways-in-use](https://github.com/L13w/runways-in-use) - Parses D-ATIS to determine active runway configurations at US airports, available via API.

## Training and reference

- [Visual Vectoring](https://visualvectoring.com/) - Vectoring and separation courses with an accompanying simulator.

## Community platforms

- [SimAware TRACON](https://github.com/vatsimnetwork/simaware-tracon-project) - Open TRACON boundary definitions used by the VATSIM network.

## Adjacent

Not air traffic control, but close enough that people keep asking.
